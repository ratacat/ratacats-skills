# Lens Pack: Ideation

Mode: explore. Targets: topics, product concepts, business ideas, creative briefs, essays-to-be, strategies, names, features, open questions — anything where the ask is "generate" or "what am I missing", not "verify".

Probe grammar: directional questions. Each probe names a direction and asks what lives there. Closure is on yield: `fertile` requires captured material distinct from every other probe's yield.

## Lenses

Generate 1-5 probes per applicable lens unless the user requests a different distribution.

1. Adjacent possible — nearest neighbors of the current idea; one-step mutations
2. First principles — rebuild from raw constraints, ignoring how it's done today
3. Anti-goal — design the worst version deliberately; invert each flaw into a candidate
4. Audience rotation — re-aim the idea at a different user, buyer, culture, or skill level
5. Resource inversion — assume 100x budget; assume zero budget; keep what survives both
6. Form transplant — force the idea into a different medium, format, or business model
7. Historical echo — precedents, failed attempts, and what changed since they failed
8. Cross-domain steal — how an unrelated field solves the structurally same problem
9. Subtraction — remove the feature, step, or assumption everyone treats as essential
10. Amplification — take the smallest promising element and make it the whole thing
11. Friction map — where the current framing produces pain, boredom, or drop-off
12. Taboo / unfashionable — angles dismissed for social rather than substantive reasons
13. Time capsule — the version built 20 years ago; the version built 20 years from now
14. Recombination bench — cross the two most fertile yields from earlier lenses

## Yield Capture

For every `fertile` probe, capture yield as concrete items: a named idea, a one-line framing, an option with its tradeoff, a risk with its trigger. "This direction seems interesting" is not yield. Cluster the final yield by theme, lead with the strongest clusters, and list `parked` directions at the end for future rounds.

## Operator Hints

Ideation lenses already embed inversion, analogy, and scale moves. The operators that add the most beyond the pack:
- **Extremization** — push the favorite candidate to absurdity; note where it breaks and what the break reveals.
- **Stakeholder rotation** — the skeptic, the power user, the person who profits from the status quo.
- **Modality shift** — sketch the idea as a diagram or a number; the gap between tellings is often a new direction.
